Department Vision & Objectives

Our commitment to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation in Computer Science and Information Technology.

Institution Vision

To be a premier Institution in Engineering, Technology and Management with competency, values and social consciousness.

Institution Mission

IM1: Provide high quality academic programs, training activities and research facilities.

IM2: Promote continuous Industry-Institute interaction for employability, entrepreneurship, and research aptitude.

IM3: Contribute to the economic and technological development of the region, state, and nation.

Department Vision

To become a prominent department empowering students to achieve distinction in academic and professional careers in computer science and information technology.

Department Mission

DM1: Impart professional education to advance intellectual ability and software engineering competence.

DM2: Build engineers with high collaborative ability, teamwork, leadership, and communication skills.

DM3: Uplift innovation, product development, and research to serve industrial and societal commitments.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

PEO1 — Professional Mastery: Graduates demonstrate technical competency and leadership to build impactful software solutions in top global organizations.

PEO2 — Continuous Learning: Graduates pursue lifelong learning, advanced research, and innovative problem-solving in emerging technologies.

PEO3 — Ethical Leadership: Graduates exhibit high professional ethics and societal responsibility towards sustainable engineering solutions.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

PSO1 — Algorithms & Software: Design, analyze, and implement robust algorithms and software systems using modern programming paradigms.

PSO2 — Systems & Security: Apply computing skills in network architecture, cloud systems, information security, and enterprise database management.

PSO3 — Product Innovation: Develop innovative applications and contribute to open-source communities meeting real-world industry demands.

Program Outcomes (POs) Summary

PO1 — Engineering Knowledge: Apply mathematics, natural science, computing, and engineering fundamentals to solve complex problems.

PO2 — Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, review literature, and analyze engineering challenges reaching substantiated conclusions.

PO3 — Design of Solutions: Design creative solutions for complex engineering problems considering public health, safety, and environment.

PO4 — Conduct Investigations: Use research-based methods, experimental design, and data interpretation to provide valid conclusions.

PO5 — Modern Tool Usage: Create, select, and apply modern IT tools and engineering software with an understanding of limitations.

PO6 — The Engineer & Society: Apply reasoning to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues relevant to professional practice.

PO7 — Environment & Sustainability: Understand the impact of engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts.

PO8 — Ethics: Commit to professional ethics, responsibilities, and norms of engineering practice.

PO9 — Individual & Team Work: Function effectively as an individual, team member, or leader in multidisciplinary settings.

PO10 — Communication: Communicate effectively through reports, documentation, and impactful presentations.

PO11 — Project Management: Apply engineering and management principles to lead projects in multidisciplinary environments.

PO12 — Lifelong Learning: Recognize the need for and engage in independent, lifelong learning in the broadest context of technological change.

4-Year Success Plan

The Engineering Placement Roadmap

Follow these structured milestones from Year 1 to Year 4 to secure top product and enterprise software roles.

1

Year 1: Programming Foundation & CS Core

Master C / C++ / Python, Basic Problem Solving & Math Foundations

Coding Language

  • Master syntax, memory pointers, recursion & OOP concepts in C++ or Java.
  • Solve 50+ basic problems on HackerRank / CodeChef.

Development Basics

  • Learn Git version control and setup your GitHub profile.
  • Understand HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript fundamentals.

Academic Target

  • Maintain a GPA of 8.0+ across Semesters 1 and 2.
  • Participate in departmental coding club activities.
2

Year 2: Data Structures, Algorithms & Full-Stack

Conquer Arrays, Trees, Graphs, Dynamic Programming & Build Full-Stack Apps

DSA Mastery

  • Arrays, Strings, LinkedLists, Stacks, Queues, Binary Trees & Graphs.
  • Solve 150+ LeetCode problems (LeetCode 75 Roadmap).

Project Development

  • Build 2 full-stack projects (React/Node.js or Python/FastAPI with PostgreSQL).
  • Deploy projects live on Vercel / Render with public GitHub repos.

Hackathons

  • Participate in Smart India Hackathon (SIH) and college fests.
  • Contribute to open-source repositories during Hacktoberfest.
3

Year 3: Core CS Concepts & Competitive Coding

DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, System Design & Internships

CS Fundamentals

  • Revise DBMS (SQL, Normalization, ACID), OS (Concurrency, Memory), and CN (TCP/IP).
  • Prepare concise interview flashcards for OOPs principles.

Advanced DSA

  • Master Dynamic Programming, Trie, Segment Trees, and Greedy algorithms.
  • Complete Striver SDE Sheet / NeetCode 150.

Summer Internships

  • Apply for summer technical internships via LinkedIn & campus drives.
  • Prepare ATS-friendly 1-page resume using LaTeX/Overleaf.
4

Year 4: Placement Drives & Mock Interviews

Company-Specific Testing, System Design, Behavioral HR & Offers

Mock Interviews

  • Conduct peer mock interviews on Pramp / Interviewing.io.
  • Practice STAR method for Behavioral & Leadership questions.

Company Test Prep

  • Aptitude & Logical reasoning practice on IndiaBix.
  • Company-specific previous papers for TCS NQT, Cognizant GenC, Amazon, etc.

Final Placement

  • Attend on-campus & off-campus drives with verified documentation.
  • Secure offer letters and negotiate compensation packages.
